===================================================================
HOW TO PLAY THE NUMBER GUESSING GAME INTERACTIVELY
===================================================================

To play the game yourself, follow these steps:

1. Open your terminal/command prompt

2. Navigate to the Day2 folder:
   cd Day2

3. Run the game:
   python number_guessing_game.py

4. You will see a menu:
   *** Welcome to Number Guessing Game!
   
   ==================================================
   MAIN MENU
   ==================================================
   1. Play Classic Mode (1-100, 10 attempts)
   2. Play with Difficulty Levels
   3. Exit
   ==================================================
   
   Select option (1-3):

5. Type 1 and press Enter for Classic Mode

6. The game will tell you it's thinking of a number between 1-100

7. You type your guess (example: 50) and press Enter

8. The game will tell you:
   - ">> Too low! Try higher" if your guess is too low
   - "<< Too high! Try lower" if your guess is too high
   - "*** CONGRATULATIONS!" if you guessed correctly

9. Keep guessing until you win or run out of attempts (10 total)

10. After the game, it will ask if you want to play again
